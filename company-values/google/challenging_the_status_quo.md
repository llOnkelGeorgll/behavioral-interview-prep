# Challenging the Status Quo

**Definition:**
Proactively identifying processes, designs, or structures that are inefficient, outdated, or broken, and taking the initiative to propose and implement better solutions.

### What they look for (Behavioral Indicators)
- **Innovation and Improvement:** Questioning "why we do things this way" and seeking creative ways to improve outcomes.
- **Calculated Risks:** Taking smart, researched risks to try new things, even if failure is a possibility.
- **Courage to Speak Up:** Presenting constructive feedback and new ideas politely but firmly to stakeholders, including leadership.
- **Ownership:** Driving the change you want to see, rather than just complaining about the problem.

### Sample Interview Questions
- Tell me about a time you identified a major bottleneck or inefficiency in your team or company. How did you address it?
- Describe a situation where you took a calculated risk to innovate. What was the outcome?
- Give me an example of a time you challenged a standard practice or decision. How did you influence others?
