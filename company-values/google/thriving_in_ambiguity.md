# Thriving in Ambiguity

**Definition:**
The ability to remain calm, focused, and productive when project requirements are unclear, changes occur frequently, or problems are poorly defined. It is the capacity to make decisions and progress with incomplete information.

### What they look for (Behavioral Indicators)
- **Comfort with Change:** Adapting quickly to shifts in priorities, scope, or team structures.
- **Decision-Making:** Making logical progress and deciding on a course of action even when data is missing or requirements are vague.
- **Problem Solving:** Creating structure out of chaos, establishing logical frameworks, and taking ownership of ill-defined challenges.
- **Self-Directed Action:** Proactively seeking information and driving results without waiting for detailed guidelines or hand-holding.

### Sample Interview Questions
- Tell me about a time you had to make an important decision with very limited or incomplete information.
- Describe a situation where your project requirements were constantly changing. How did you manage it?
- Tell me about a time you had to solve a complex problem with completely unclear or vague requirements.
