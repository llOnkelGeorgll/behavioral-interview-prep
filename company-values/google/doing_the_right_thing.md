# Doing the Right Thing

**Definition:**
Conducting oneself with integrity, prioritizing ethical decision-making, and looking out for users, coworkers, and the community—even when it means taking a more difficult path.

### What they look for (Behavioral Indicators)
- **Ethical Integrity:** Making decisions that align with high moral standards, even under commercial pressure.
- **Supporting Coworkers:** Fostering a supportive, non-condescending, and safe environment; helping teammates and promoting equity.
- **Transparency:** Communicating honestly and transparently, avoiding manipulation or shortcuts.
- **User Integrity:** Protecting user privacy, data, and experiences as a primary duty.

### Sample Interview Questions
- Tell me about a time you had to make a decision that was ethical rather than convenient or profitable.
- Describe a situation where you had to push back against a decision because you felt it was not the right thing to do.
- Tell me about a time you noticed a coworker or peer struggling, and how you supported them.
