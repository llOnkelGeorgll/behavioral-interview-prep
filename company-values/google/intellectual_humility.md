# Intellectual Humility & Valuing Feedback

**Definition:**
Having the self-awareness to admit when you are wrong, valuing diverse opinions over personal ego, and actively seeking and integrating feedback to improve yourself and your work.

### What they look for (Behavioral Indicators)
- **Ego Management:** Placing team success and user needs above personal pride or being "right."
- **Feedback Loop:** Welcoming critical feedback, responding to it constructively (rather than defensively), and taking concrete actions based on it.
- **Admitting Mistakes:** Promptly taking ownership of failures, correcting them transparently, and sharing the lessons learned.
- **Curiosity:** Valuing other perspectives, listening intently, and collaborating to build a better collective solution.

### Sample Interview Questions
- Tell me about a time you received critical feedback that was difficult to hear. How did you react and what did you do?
- Describe a situation where you made a significant mistake at work. How did you handle it, and what was the outcome?
- Tell me about a time when you realized a teammate had a better approach to a problem than yours. How did you handle it?
