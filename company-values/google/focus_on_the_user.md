# Focus on the User

**Definition:**
Google's primary core value: "Focus on the user and all else will follow." It means prioritizing user benefit, usability, and experience above all other commercial metrics.

### What they look for (Behavioral Indicators)
- **User Empathy:** Understanding the user's needs, frustrations, and goals on a deep level.
- **Prioritization:** Ensuring that technical designs and business decisions serve the user's long-term interest.
- **Advocacy:** Speaking up for the user in discussions about scope, timing, or monetization.
- **Continuous Feedback:** Collecting and acting on user feedback to iteratively refine product quality.

### Sample Interview Questions
- Tell me about a time you had to balance a commercial metric (like profit or timing) with the user's experience. What did you prioritize?
- Describe a situation where user feedback drastically changed your product or engineering direction.
- Tell me about a time you went above and beyond to solve a customer or user problem.
